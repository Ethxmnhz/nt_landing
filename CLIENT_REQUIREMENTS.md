# Photography & Services Checklist

Here is the specific list of photography assets and service details needed for the **Naturenergy** website.

## 1. Required Photography List

Please provide high-quality photos. For website use, landscape (horizontal) orientation is usually best for backgrounds, while a mix of portrait and landscape is good for other sections.

- [ ] **Main Hero Image (Home Page)**
  - *Description:* This is the first thing visitors see. It should evoke a sense of calm, grounding, and renewal (e.g., a serene natural environment, a peaceful clinic space, or a soft, abstract texture). It needs enough negative (empty) space so we can comfortably place the main headline text over it.

- [ ] **Founder / "About" Portraits**
  - *Description:* 1 to 2 warm, approachable, and professional photos of the founder. These should convey empathy and trust. Ideally, one looking directly at the camera and another in a more candid, relaxed pose.

- [ ] **Chakra Energy Healing Image**
  - *Description:* A photo representing energy work or your healing environment. This could be a picture of a treatment room, hands resting gently, or a calming setup (e.g., crystals, soft lighting) to visually explain energy rebalancing.

- [ ] **Physical Movement Image**
  - *Description:* A photo showing gentle, restorative physical movement. This could be you or a client engaging in light stretching, grounding exercises, or mindful movement in a peaceful setting.

- [ ] **Emotional Support / Consultation Image**
  - *Description:* A photo representing connection and support. This could be an image of two chairs facing each other in your consultation space, or a candid shot of you actively listening and taking notes.

- [ ] **Secondary Backgrounds / Ambient Shots (Optional but recommended)**
  - *Description:* 2 to 3 photos of natural elements (leaves, water, light flares) or your workspace details (a warm lamp, a cup of tea). These are used to break up text sections on pages like the FAQ, Packages, and Contact page.
